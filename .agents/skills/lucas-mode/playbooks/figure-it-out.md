# Figure it out

Design a task-specific workflow when no narrow playbook fits. The workflow is the first artifact.

1. Ground the systems and state a falsifiable completion predicate. Quantify the units, unknowns, and constraints.
2. Sequence the riskiest uncertainty first. Capture a baseline and establish the verification method before changing the artifact.
3. Use [Architect and arena](../references/agent-workflows.md#architect-and-arena) for consequential unresolved designs. A settled mechanical transformation does not need another design contest.
4. Write phases with explicit input, owned output, acceptance check, and dependency. Identify what can fan out through [T3 orchestration](../references/t3-orchestration.md).
5. Execute each unit as a hypothesis: make the smallest justified change, observe the result, keep or revert. Pair substantive delegated changes with independent review.
6. Keep the [decision trail](../references/decision-trail.md) as work proceeds, including pivots and failed checks.
7. Verify the whole deliverable against the original predicate. Turn demonstrated recurring mistakes into structural checks when that adds value.

Honor a plan-only request or an explicit checkpoint. Otherwise continue the authorized execution after presenting the approach. Report the designed phases, consequential choices, verification, and unresolved limits.
